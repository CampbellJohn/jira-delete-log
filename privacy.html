Deleted Issue Log: Privacy Policy

Publisher: Algorithmic Enterprises
Contact: algorithmicenterprises@gmail.com
Effective date: 30 September 2026

What the app does

Deleted Issue Log is a Forge app for Jira Cloud. Whenever an issue is deleted on your site, it saves a short record of that issue so that Jira admins (and project admins, for their own projects) can see what was deleted.

What data it stores

The app stores one record per deleted issue, built from the deletion event Jira sends to the app:

  • the issue's id, key, summary, type, status, due date, fix version names and created date
  • the project id and key
  • the Atlassian account IDs of the assignee, reporter, creator and the user who deleted the issue
  • the time of deletion

It does not store names, email addresses, issue descriptions, comments, attachments or custom fields. Account IDs are shown in the app's pages, where Jira resolves them to display names for viewers who are allowed to see them.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in Atlassian's Forge storage (Key-Value Store) for your site, and it is processed only inside Atlassian's Forge platform. The app makes no calls to external servers, and the publisher does not run any server or database of its own. The publisher cannot read your site's stored records.

Who can see it

  • The admin page: users with Jira administrator access.
  • The project settings page: users who can administer that project. It shows only that project's deletions.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept until the app is uninstalled. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes its Forge storage for your site under Atlassian's data retention policy for Forge apps. Once a week the app reports the account IDs it stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting API. If Atlassian reports an account as closed, the app removes that account ID from every record.

Sharing

The publisher does not sell, share or transfer your data. The app has no analytics and no tracking.

Atlassian

The Atlassian Privacy Policy also applies to your use of Jira and the Forge platform: https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy
