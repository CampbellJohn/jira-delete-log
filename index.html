FAQ

Does it capture issues deleted before I installed it?
No. Jira only notifies the app of deletions that happen after installation.

Why is the description missing?
Jira's deletion event doesn't include the description, comments or attachments, so the app can't capture them.

Can it restore a deleted issue?
Not automatically. The record gives you the key, summary, type, status, people and dates, so you can recreate the issue by hand.

Who can see the log?
Jira admins see all projects (Apps → Deleted Issue Log). Project admins see their own project's log (Project settings → Deleted issues).

Does any data leave Atlassian?
No. The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge and makes no external calls. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove the data?
Uninstall the app. Atlassian then deletes its stored data under its Forge retention policy.

Security

Where it runs: Entirely on Atlassian Forge. The app has no servers of its own and makes no calls outside Atlassian (no egress permissions).

Access it requests: read:jira-work (to read the deleted issue's fields and show the log) and storage:app (to keep the records in the app's own Forge storage). It can't create, edit or delete anything in your Jira.

Personal data: Records store Atlassian account IDs. Each week the app checks them with Atlassian's personal data reporting API and erases the IDs of any accounts Atlassian reports as closed.

Certifications: None. The app doesn't hold SOC 2, ISO 27001 or similar certifications.

Reporting a vulnerability: Email algorithmicenterprises@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject. We aim to acknowledge within 2 business days and to fix confirmed issues as quickly as we can. Please don't publish details until we've had a chance to fix them. There's no bug bounty.
